隐私政策

你记录的内容只保存在你的设备上。嘬一口没有账号系统、没有广告、不做跨应用追踪。

  • 你记录的饮品、食物、价格、照片和贴纸只保存在你的设备上（App Group 本地数据库）。
  • 如果你在设置中打开 iCloud 同步，数据保存在你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库中，开发者无法访问。
  • 拍照抠图使用 Apple 设备端的 Vision 框架完成，照片不会上传到任何服务器，也不使用生成式人工智能。
  • 相机和相册权限只用于制作贴纸；通知权限只用于你开启的提醒。
  • 购买由 Apple 处理，我们收不到你的支付信息。
  • 导出（CSV / 备份文件）由你自己通过系统分享面板保存或发送。
  • 匿名使用统计：我们使用 Google Firebase Analytics 和 Crashlytics 了解哪些功能被使用、App 是否崩溃（例如“打开了统计页”“完成了一次记录”）。统计不包含你记录的饮品、价格、照片或备注，不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不用于广告。中国大陆用户需先同意；所有用户都可以在「设置 › 帮助改进」随时关闭。

删除 App 即删除设备上的全部数据；iCloud 中的数据可在「设置 › Apple 账户 › iCloud › 管理存储空间」中删除。

Privacy Policy

What you log stays on your device. Sippo has no accounts, no ads and no cross-app tracking.

  • Your drinks, meals, prices, photos and stickers are stored only on your device.
  • If you turn on iCloud sync, data is stored in your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot access.
  • Photo cutouts are made on device with Apple’s Vision framework. Photos are never uploaded and no generative AI is used.
  • Camera and photo access are used only to make stickers; notifications only for reminders you turn on.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; we never receive payment details.
  • Exports (CSV / backup) are saved or shared by you through the system share sheet.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: we use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to learn which features are used and whether the app crashes (for example “opened Stats”, “logged an entry”). They never include your drinks, prices, photos or notes, do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and are not used for ads. Users in mainland China are asked first; everyone can turn this off anytime in Settings › Help improve.

Deleting the app deletes all data on the device; iCloud data can be removed in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage.
